Introduction

Keeping your user profile up to date ensures that your rental business information is accurate and that guests can easily connect with you through your social channels.

This guide demonstrates how to access and modify your personal profile settings in FavHost. Updating your profile is an essential first step for new hosts and a routine task for experienced managers who need to refresh their contact details, bio, or social media links. By the end of this tutorial, you will know how to navigate to your profile, edit your personal information, and save changes to your public-facing host persona.

Accessing Your Profile

Before you can make any changes, you must log in to your FavHost account and navigate to the profile management area.

Step 1: Log in to FavHost

Start by visiting the FavHost homepage and clicking the Login button in the top right corner. Enter your registered email and password in the provided fields and click Login to access your dashboard.

Step 2: Navigate to the Profile Page

Once you are on the main dashboard, look for your user avatar or the Profile dropdown menu in the top right corner of the navigation bar. Click on it and select Profile from the list of options. This will take you to your personal profile summary page.

Updating Profile Information

The profile summary page displays your current bio, contact details, and account status. To make changes, you will need to enter the editing mode.

Step 1: Enter Edit Mode

On the profile summary page, locate the Edit button in the top right corner, just below the main navigation bar. Clicking this will unlock the fields and allow you to update your information.

Step 2: Modify Personal Details

In the Profile editor, you can update various text fields and dropdown menus:

  • Name & Contact: Update your first name, last name, and phone number.

  • Location: Use the dropdowns to select your country and state.

  • Preferences: Set your preferred currency (e.g., USD) and primary language (e.g., English).

  • About: Rewrite your personal bio to better introduce yourself to potential guests.

Providing a detailed "About" section helps build trust with guests by sharing your experience and commitment to hospitality.

Managing Media and Social Links

Beyond text-based information, you can enhance your profile by adding professional images and connecting your social media presence.

Step 1: Upload Profile and ID Photos

On the right side of the editor, you will find upload areas for your profile photo and government-issued ID.

  1. Click on the Upload Profile Photo box to open your computer's file browser.

  2. Select a clear, professional image of yourself.

  3. Repeat the process for any required ID Document or local authority permission uploads.

Step 2: Update Social Media Links

At the bottom of the page, you can link your various social media profiles. Enter the full URLs for your Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, or YouTube accounts. You can also provide a WhatsApp number to facilitate direct communication.

Step 3: Save Your Changes

Once you have finished updating all fields and uploading documents, click the orange Save button located at the top right of the edit panel. A "Profile updated successfully" notification will appear at the top of the screen to confirm the changes have been applied.

Conclusion

Keeping your profile current is key to maintaining a professional image on FavHost. With your contact details updated and your social media accounts linked, you are now better equipped to communicate with guests and manage your rental business effectively. As a next step, consider reviewing your property listings to ensure your updated profile information aligns with your current offerings.

FAQ

  • How do I change my login password?

    You can find the "Change password" link on your profile summary page, right next to your account's subscription status.

  • Can I use a different email address for notifications?

    Yes, you can update your email address in the Profile edit mode; however, remember that this will also change the email you use to log in to the platform.

  • Why do I need to upload a government-issued ID?

    Providing a valid ID helps FavHost verify your identity, which increases trust and security for both hosts and guests within the marketplace.

  • Where do my social media links appear?

    Your social media links are typically displayed on your public host profile, allowing potential guests to find more information about your business.

  • Is there a limit to the file size for profile photo uploads?

    While FavHost accepts standard image formats like JPG and PNG, it is best to use a high-quality photo with a manageable file size (under 5MB) to ensure a quick upload.

  • Can I change the currency after I have already saved my profile?

    Yes, you can return to the Edit mode at any time to change your preferred currency or any other personal setting.

  • What should I do if the "Save" button doesn't work?

    Ensure all required fields (marked with an asterisk) are filled out and that your uploaded files are in the correct format. If issues persist, try refreshing the page.
