Introduction

Efficiently managing short-term rental bookings requires a clear view of your guest data and payment schedules. The Favhost dashboard provides a comprehensive suite of tools to track, edit, and report on all your active and upcoming reservations.

This guide walks you through the core reservation management workflows in Favhost, including viewing detailed reservation records, editing guest information, generating guest receipts, and exporting filtered reservation reports. Whether you are a property owner or a manager, these steps will help you maintain accurate records and provide professional documentation to your guests.

Managing Reservation Records

The Reservations module is the central hub for tracking your occupancy. From here, you can drill down into specific booking details to review payment schedules and guest requirements.

Step 1: Access the Reservations List

Navigate to the top header and click on Reservations. This view organizes your bookings into several tabs: Past, Recent Booking, Upcoming, Executed, and No Show. By default, you can see a summary of each guest, including their name, check-in/check-out dates, and the assigned property.

Step 2: View Detailed Reservation Information

To see the full breakdown of a booking, find the guest in the list and click the three-dot menu icon on the far right of their entry. Select Reservation details. This opens a comprehensive view containing the Guest Details, Payment Schedule (showing due dates and amounts for deposits and fees), and a Payment Summary of the total stay.

Step 3: Modify Existing Reservations

If guest details or booking notes need to be updated, return to the Reservations list and select Edit from the three-dot menu. The Edit Reservation interface allows you to update Guest Details, change the Booking source, or adjust the Number of guests.

Changes made in the Edit Reservation screen are reflected immediately across the dashboard and in any generated reports.

Generating Guest Documentation and Receipts

Providing guests with professional documentation is essential for clear communication regarding their stay and financial obligations. Favhost allows you to generate and export these documents with a few clicks.

Step 1: Open the Guest Receipt

From the main Reservations list, locate the specific booking you need a receipt for. Open the three-dot menu on the right and select Guest Receipt. This generates a branded receipt modal.

Step 2: Print or Save as PDF

Inside the receipt modal, you will see a summary of the stay, including the host information, price breakdown (cleaning fee, refundable deposit, application fee), and payment status. To save a copy for your records or send it to the guest, click the Print icon at the top of the modal.

Exporting Reservation Reports

For high-level management and bookkeeping, you may need to export data for multiple reservations at once. The Print Report feature serves this purpose by consolidating booking data and guest documents into a single file.

Step 1: Filter Your View

Before generating a report, use the status tabs (e.g., Upcoming, Executed) to filter the list to the specific set of reservations you want to include. For example, selecting the Upcoming tab will isolate bookings that haven't started yet.

Step 2: Generate the Print Report

Click the Print Report button located at the top right of the Reservations page. A dropdown will appear with options such as All Reservations, Current Month, or Upcoming Reservations. Choosing Upcoming Reservations will open a new formatted view ready for export.

Step 3: Review and Export the Consolidated Report

The generated report includes a summary table of Upcoming Reservations with columns for reservation ID, guest name, property, check-in/out dates, and status. Below the table, the report also compiles Guest photos & documents, such as ID uploads or profile pictures, providing a complete packet of information.

When exporting reports, you can use the Print / Save as PDF button at the top of the report preview to generate a digital file for offline use or physical filing.

Conclusion

Mastering the reservation management tools in Favhost ensures that your rental business operates smoothly and professionally. By utilizing the detailed reservation views, editing capabilities, and guest receipt generation, you can provide high-quality service to your guests while maintaining precise financial records. The ability to export filtered reports further simplifies your administrative tasks, giving you a clear overview of your performance and upcoming occupancy at any time.

As a next step, consider exploring the Calendar view to manage your property availability alongside these reservation records.

FAQ

  • How do I find a specific guest's payment history?

    Navigate to the Reservations tab, find the guest, and click on Reservation details. The Payment Schedule section provides a complete history of due dates and amounts for that specific booking.

  • Can I update a guest's contact information after they have booked?

    Yes. Use the Edit option in the three-dot menu for any reservation to modify guest names, phone numbers, and email addresses.

  • What is the difference between the 'Executed' and 'No Show' tabs?

    The Executed tab contains completed stays where the guest checked in and out as planned. The No Show tab is for reservations where the guest failed to arrive.

  • Can I export a report for just one specific property?

    While the Print Report feature defaults to the current filtered list, you can use the search bar or property filters (if available in your specific dashboard configuration) to isolate a property before clicking Print Report.

  • Are guest documents included in the printed reports?

    Yes, the consolidated reports automatically include guest photos and uploaded identification documents at the bottom of the summary table.

  • How can I send a receipt to a guest?

    Open the Guest Receipt from the reservation's menu, then use the Print icon to save the receipt as a PDF. You can then email this PDF file directly to your guest.

  • What should I do if a reservation is incorrectly categorized?

    You can use the Edit function to adjust booking details, or use status-specific actions in the menu (like marking a no-show) to move the reservation to the correct tab.
