Introduction

Managing a property often requires a team. In Favhost, the Manage Co-host feature allows you to delegate tasks and provide system access to partners without sharing your primary account credentials.

In this guide, you will learn how to access the co-host management interface, add new team members with specific login credentials, and manage existing co-host records. This workflow is essential for property managers who need to scale their operations or provide temporary access to cleaning and maintenance staff.

Accessing Co-host Settings

Before you can add a team member, you must navigate to the specific management section within your Favhost profile. This area serves as your central hub for all secondary user accounts.

1

Navigate to the Co-host Manager

From your main Profile page, look for the Attachments section at the bottom of the interface. Here, you will find several management options. Click on Manage Co-host to open the dedicated dashboard for secondary users.

Adding a New Co-host

Adding a co-host involves creating a unique identity for your partner within the system. You will define their login email and a specific password for them to use.

2

Open the Add Co-host Dialog

Once on the Manage Co-host page, you will see a list of any existing partners. To add someone new, click the + Add Co-host button located in the top-right corner of the table.

3

Enter Partner Details

A modal will appear titled Add Co-host. You must fill in the following fields to create the account:

  • EMAIL: Enter the partner's professional email address.

  • COHOST PASSWORD: Set a secure password for their initial login.

  • FULL NAME: Provide the individual's name for easy identification in your list.

  • PHONE: Include their contact number for administrative records.

4

Save the New User

Review the information for accuracy. Once confirmed, click the orange Save Co-host button at the bottom of the modal. A green success notification will appear at the top of the screen confirming the user has been added.

Managing Existing Co-hosts

Once a co-host is added, you can modify their details or remove their access entirely as your staffing needs change.

5

Edit Co-host Information

If a team member changes their phone number or if you need to update their name, click the pencil icon located in the Action column for that specific user. This re-opens the detail modal, now titled Edit Co-host.

After making your changes, click Save Co-host. A green notification will confirm the update was successful.

6

Delete a Co-host

To revoke access, click the trash can icon in the Action column. A confirmation dialog will appear to ensure you don't accidentally delete a user.

7

Confirm Removal

Click the red Delete button in the confirmation modal. The user will be removed from your table immediately, and a final confirmation banner will appear at the top of the dashboard.

Deleting a co-host is permanent. Once deleted, the user will no longer be able to log in with those credentials, and you will need to re-add them if they require access again in the future.

Conclusion

By managing your co-hosts directly through your Favhost profile, you maintain a secure and organized property management team. You can now add new partners, update their contact information, and revoke access as needed.

As a next step, ensure your newly added co-hosts have the necessary Permission Documents uploaded in the same Attachments section of your profile to complete their onboarding.

FAQ

  • Can I assign different permissions to each co-host?

    The "Manage Co-host" screen primarily focuses on login credentials. Specific functional permissions are often managed through the "Permission Documents" section or within specific listing settings.

  • What happens if a co-host forgets their password?

    You can click the pencil icon to edit their profile and set a new password for them manually, then click Save Co-host to apply the change.

  • Can a co-host see my primary account billing information?

    No, co-host accounts are secondary and typically restricted from viewing the primary owner's subscription and billing details found on the main profile.

  • Is there a limit to how many co-hosts I can add?

    Most plans allow for multiple co-hosts, though you should check your specific Favhost subscription details if you are managing a very large team.

  • Will a co-host be notified when I delete their account?

    The system revokes their access immediately. While they may not receive an automated email for the deletion itself, they will no longer be able to log in.

  • Can I re-use an email address for a different co-host?

    Each co-host must have a unique email address associated with their account. If you need to re-use an email, you must first delete the existing co-host record associated with it.
