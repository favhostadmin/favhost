Introduction

Efficiently track your vacation rental income and expenses to maintain a healthy bottom line. This guide demonstrates how to navigate financial reports and manage property-specific costs in FavHost.

Maintaining accurate financial records is essential for property managers and hosts using FavHost. By utilizing the built-in accounting tools, you can view detailed revenue breakdowns by listing or channel and record specific expenses—like utilities or cleaning fees—against your properties. This ensures your net profit calculations are always up to date and reflective of your actual business performance.

Navigating Financial Reports

FavHost provides comprehensive views of your earnings, allowing you to filter data by property or timeframe to understand where your revenue is coming from.

Revenue by Listing

The Revenue by Listing report offers a granular look at how individual properties are performing. You can see a month-by-month breakdown of check-ins, nights stayed, and total revenue for every listing in your portfolio.

Income, Expenses, and Profit Tracking

To get a high-level view of your business's financial health, navigate to the Accounting & Expense Tracking section. This consolidated view presents a table of income from all sources alongside a detailed breakdown of expenses, culminating in a net profit summary at the bottom.

Use the year selector at the top of the accounting pages to quickly switch between different fiscal years for historical comparisons.

Managing Property Expenses

Keeping track of out-of-pocket costs is vital for accurate profit reporting. FavHost allows you to record expenses, categorize them, and even attach receipts for digital record-keeping.

1

Step 1: Open the Add Expense Dialog

In the Accounting and expense tracking section, look for the orange + Add Expense button at the top left of the main panel. Clicking this opens a modal where you can input the details of your transaction.

1

Step 2: Categorize and Value the Expense

Start by selecting a Category from the dropdown menu, such as Utilities, Supplies, or Maintenance. This helps organize your reports. Then, enter the specific Amount of the expense.

1

Step 3: Choosing date and listing

Assign the expense to a specific date using the calendar picker. Importantly, you should select the Listing this expense applies to from the dropdown menu. If it's a general cost, you can leave it as "Business-wide."

1

Step 4: Add Notes and Attachments

For better clarity later, add a descriptive Note (e.g., "Electric bill"). You can also click Choose File under the Attachment section to upload a photo or PDF of the receipt. Once finished, click Save Expense.

Attaching digital receipts directly to expenses in FavHost can significantly simplify your tax preparation and audit processes.

Modifying and Deleting Expenses

Financial records sometimes need adjustments. FavHost makes it easy to update existing entries or remove errors.

Updating an Existing Entry

If you need to change the details of an expense, locate it in the list on the left side of the accounting page. Click the Edit link next to the entry to reopen the details modal, make your changes, and click Update Expense.

Deleting a Record

To completely remove an expense, click the Delete link next to the entry in the list. A confirmation dialog will appear to ensure you don't accidentally lose data; click Delete again to finalize the removal.

Conclusion

With FavHost's accounting tools, you can transition from simple booking management to full-scale financial oversight. By regularly navigating your revenue reports and meticulously tracking property expenses, you gain a clear understanding of your net profit. This data-driven approach allows you to make informed decisions about property investments and operational costs. FAQ

  • Where can I see my total profit across all listings?

    You can find this at the bottom of the Accounting and expense tracking page. Scroll past the Income and Expenses tables to view the "Profit by month" chart and the "Net Profit" row in the summary table.

  • Can I assign an expense to multiple properties at once?

    Expenses are typically linked to a single listing to ensure accurate property-level reporting. For overhead costs that apply to your entire portfolio, select the "Business-wide" option in the Listing dropdown.

  • What file types can I upload as expense attachments?

    FavHost supports common image and document formats, such as PNG, JPG, and PDF, allowing you to easily store digital copies of receipts and invoices.

  • Does deleting an expense also delete the attached file?

    Yes, when you confirm the deletion of an expense record, the associated attachment is also removed from the system. Ensure you have a backup if the receipt is needed for other purposes.

  • How often should I update my expense records?

    It is best practice to record expenses as they occur or on a weekly basis. This keeps your "Profit by month" charts accurate and saves time during month-end or year-end reconciliations.

  • Can I export these financial reports for my accountant?

    While this guide focuses on on-screen management, the tabular format of the Income and Expense pages is designed for easy review. Look for export icons (often near the year selector) if you need to share data externally.

  • What happens if I categorize an expense incorrectly?

    You can easily fix this by clicking the "Edit" link next to the expense in your list. Simply select the correct category from the dropdown menu and click "Update Expense" to save the change.
