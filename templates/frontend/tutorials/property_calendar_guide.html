Introduction

Managing a property portfolio requires a clear view of bookings, availability, and financial performance across various platforms. FavHost provides a centralized calendar system to streamline these operations.

This guide demonstrates how to navigate and utilize the FavHost calendar to manage short-term rental listings efficiently. You will learn how to switch between different calendar views—including Timeline, Month, Week, and List—to gain insights into your property performance and upcoming guest arrivals. This article is intended for property managers and hosts using FavHost as their primary reservation management platform.

Navigating the Calendar Interface

The Calendar is the heart of the FavHost dashboard, offering multiple ways to visualize your data. Accessing the calendar allows you to see real-time occupancy and guest details at a glance.

Step 1: Access the Calendar Timeline

From the main Dashboard, locate the top navigation menu and click on Calendar. By default, this opens the Timeline view, which displays your listings as rows and dates as columns. This view is ideal for seeing overlapping bookings and identifying gaps in your schedule.

Step 2: Review Booking Details

Within the Timeline, you can hover over or click on any booking bar to see a quick summary of the reservation. This pop-up typically includes the guest's name, booking dates, total price, and the source of the reservation (e.g., Airbnb, Direct).

The timeline colors often correspond to different booking statuses or platforms, helping you visually distinguish between confirmed stays and blocked dates.

Filtering and Customizing Your View

To focus on specific data points like payments or tasks, FavHost allows you to filter the calendar content and jump to specific dates.

Step 3: Use the Calendar Filters

Located at the top of the calendar interface is the Show All dropdown menu. Click this to toggle specific layers on or off. You can choose to view only Reservations, Tasks, or Payments. This is particularly useful when you want to clean up the view to focus solely on financial milestones or housekeeping schedules.

Step 4: Change Date Ranges

To navigate through time, use the date picker or the arrow icons at the top of the calendar. You can quickly jump to a specific month or year to plan for peak seasons or review past performance. The Today button (usually represented by a circle icon or 'Today' text) will immediately return you to the current date.

Switching Calendar Views

FavHost offers several layout options to suit different management tasks. Whether you need a broad monthly overview or a granular daily schedule, you can switch views using the dropdown menu at the top right of the calendar interface.

Step 5: Utilize the Month View

The Month view provides a traditional grid layout. This is excellent for visualizing occupancy density over a 30-day period. Properties are listed on the left, and the grid fills with color-coded bars for each reservation. Hovering over a property name on the left will highlight its specific row in the grid.

Step 6: Inspect the Week and Day Views

For more detailed planning, especially regarding check-ins and check-outs, use the Week or Day views.

  • Week view: Shows a horizontal timeline for a single week, offering more space per day to read guest names.

  • Day view: Provides an hourly breakdown of the selected day. This is the best view for coordinating cleaning staff or managing arrival times.

Step 7: Analyze the List View

The List view strips away the grid entirely, presenting all events as a chronological feed. This view is highly searchable and displays key event types—such as Check-ins, Check-outs, Payments, and New Enquiries—with distinct icons and summaries.

Exporting and Printing Reports

For offline coordination or bookkeeping, you can generate professional PDF reports of your calendar data.

Step 8: Configure the Print Panel

Click the Print icon (often found in the top right actions area) to open the Print Panel. From here, you can select which calendar view you want to export (Timeline, Month, Week, etc.) and set the start date for the report.

When printing the List view, you can toggle the Show Summary Cards option to include or exclude detailed guest information on the final PDF.

Step 9: Download or Print the PDF

Once your settings are configured, click Download PDF to save the file to your device, or click Print to open your browser's system print dialog. This generates a clean, branded document containing your occupancy data, totals for bookings, and blocked dates.

Conclusion

By mastering the various calendar views and filtering tools in FavHost, you can maintain full visibility over your property portfolio. Whether you are identifying gaps in occupancy via the Timeline or coordinating daily housekeeping through the Day view, these tools are designed to simplify your hosting workflow. Your next natural step is to explore the Reservations tab to manage individual guest communications and payment details in depth.

FAQ

  • Can I filter the calendar to show only direct bookings?

    While the global filters focus on event types like payments and tasks, you can identify booking sources like Airbnb or Direct by clicking on individual reservation bars in the Timeline or Month views.

  • How do I return to today's date if I've scrolled far into the future?

    Click the "Return to current month" button, represented by a circular arrow icon next to the date picker at the top of the screen.

  • What information is included in the List view report?

    The List view report includes check-in/out dates, guest names, property names, and status updates (like payments or enquiries). You can also include detailed summary cards by toggling that option in the Print Panel.

  • Can I change the start day of the week in the calendar?

    The calendar typically defaults to Sunday or Monday depending on your regional settings. Check your account profile settings to adjust your preferred week start day.

  • How do I see which tasks are assigned to a specific day?

    Switch to the Day view and ensure that "Tasks" is selected in the "Show All" filter menu. This will display all cleaning or maintenance tasks scheduled for that specific date.

  • Does the PDF export support all calendar views?

    Yes, the Print Panel allows you to select from Timeline, Month, Week, Day, or List views to ensure your printed report matches your specific management needs.

  • What do the different colored bars on the calendar signify?

    Colors typically differentiate between confirmed reservations, manual blocks, and pending enquiries. Refer to your dashboard settings for a specific color-coding key for your account.
