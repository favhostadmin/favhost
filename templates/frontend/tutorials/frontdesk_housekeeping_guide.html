Managing Daily Operations

The FavHost dashboard serves as the central hub for your property's daily activity. From here, you can get a high-level overview of your occupancy and dive into the specifics of individual bookings to handle guest requests or check-in logistics.

Step 1: Navigating the Dashboard

Upon logging in, you are presented with a summary of your property's status. The top navigation bar allows you to switch between different modules like Bookings, Dashboard, and Housekeeping. Use the date selector in the top-left corner to view operations for a specific day, which is crucial for planning ahead or reviewing past performance.

Step 2: Accessing Guest Details

To view the specifics of a particular stay, click on a booking entry within the Check-ins or Check-outs list. This opens a detailed guest profile and itinerary view. This panel provides vital information such as the guest's contact details, vehicle information, purpose of stay, and a breakdown of their payment schedule and summary.

Reviewing the Purpose of Stay and Vehicle Information in the guest details panel helps you personalize the guest experience and manage parking resources effectively.

Tracking Housekeeping and Cleaning Status

Efficient turnover between guests depends on real-time tracking of room readiness. The Housekeeping section provides a specialized view to manage these tasks across all your listings.

Step 1: Monitoring Room Readiness

Navigate to the Housekeeping tab from the top menu. This view categorizes your properties by their current status, such as Dirty, In Progress, Clean/Ready, or Not Available. You can see at a glance how many units are ready for incoming guests and which ones still require attention.

Step 2: Customizing the Housekeeping View

Depending on your team's workflow, you may prefer different display modes. Use the view toggles on the right side of the housekeeping header to switch between a detailed list view and a more visual card view. The card view often provides large thumbnails of the properties, making it easier to identify specific units quickly.

Generating the Housekeeping Report

When it's time for your staff to begin their shift, you can generate a consolidated report. This document ensures everyone is aligned on the day's priorities without needing to access the digital dashboard.

Step 1: Previewing the Report

Click the Print icon or the report generation button located in the top-right corner of the Housekeeping dashboard. This opens the Print Panel, where you can see a live preview of the Housekeeping Report.

Step 2: Exporting and Printing

The report includes critical details for your cleaning crew, such as the number of guests expected, the bed configuration (e.g., King, Pet Friendly), and specific flags or notes. Once you have reviewed the preview, use the side panel to Download PDF or Print the document directly.

The Housekeeping Report automatically groups properties by status (e.g., Unassigned, In Progress) to help your team prioritize rooms that need immediate attention for early arrivals.

FAQ

  • How do I change the date for the Housekeeping Report?

    Use the date picker in the top-left corner of the main dashboard or housekeeping view before clicking the print icon. The report will automatically generate based on the date currently selected in your view.

  • Can I see which specific guests are checking out today?

    Yes, the main dashboard features a "Check-outs" column that lists all departures for the selected date. Clicking on any guest in this list will provide their full stay details.

  • What information is included in the 'Bed Type' column of the report?

    This column pulls information from your listing settings to show cleaners exactly how to stage the room, such as "King" or "Pet Friendly" configurations.

  • How do I update the cleaning status of a room?

    In the Housekeeping list or grid view, look for the status drop-down menu next to each property. You can manually select statuses like "Clean/Ready" or "In Progress" as work is completed.

  • Is it possible to filter the Housekeeping view by property manager?

    Yes, the dashboard allows you to filter listings by different team members or managers, which is particularly useful if you have a large portfolio of properties assigned to different staff.

  • Can I include custom notes for the cleaning crew on the report?

    Any notes added to the "Notes" section of a booking or property profile will typically be consolidated and displayed in the flags or notes section of the generated report.

  • What format is the downloaded report?

    The report is generated as a standard PDF file, making it easy to share via email, messaging apps, or to print for physical distribution.
