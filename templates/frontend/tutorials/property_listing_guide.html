Introduction

Use FavHost to streamline your property management by creating detailed, professional listings that include everything from high-quality images to specific house rules and amenities.

This guide provides a comprehensive walkthrough for property owners and managers on how to set up a new property listing in FavHost. By the end of this article, you will know how to input essential listing details, manage media uploads, configure amenities, and set pricing and house policies to attract the right guests.

Defining Core Listing Details

The first phase of creating a listing involves establishing the basic identity of your property. This includes giving it a catchy title, a clear description, and providing the logistical details guests need, such as Wi-Fi access and sleeping arrangements.

1

Initialize a New Listing To begin, navigate to the Pmsdesk dashboard. In the top-right corner of the interface, click the Create button and select Create Listing from the dropdown menu. This will open the Create Listing multi-step form where you will input all your property information.

2

Enter Basic Property Information In the Listing Details section, start by entering a descriptive Listing Title and a Nick name for internal tracking. Provide a thorough Description that highlights the unique features of your property. In the video, the user enters "Sunshine resort in downtown" as the title.

3

Configure Guest and Room Capacity Scroll down to define the property's capacity. Set the Number of Guest (max), Minimum Booking nights, and the number of Bathroom units. You also need to specify the Bed Type, such as "Queen Bed", and set the Listing Status to "Active" to ensure it's ready for booking once published.

4

Provide Connectivity and Access Details In the fields for Wi-Fi network Name and Wi-Fi Password, enter the credentials your guests will use to access the internet. Additionally, if the property or a specific room requires a code, enter it in the Room password field to ensure a smooth check-in process.

Managing Media and Amenities

Visuals are critical for conversion. In this section, you will upload photos and select the features that make your property stand out.

1

Upload Property Photos In the middle of the form, you'll find the media upload area. Click Upload Cover Photo to select the primary image guests will see first. Next, use the Upload Other Photos section to add a gallery of images. You can select multiple files from your computer and drag them into the upload box.

2

Add Video Tours and Shared Space Settings If you have a video walkthrough, paste the YouTube link for video tour in the designated field. Then, use the Private/Shared dropdown to specify if the guest is booking "An entire place" or a shared room. This setting is crucial for guest expectations.

3

Select Available Amenities On the right side of the screen, you will see a comprehensive list of Amenities. Check the boxes for all features your property provides, such as "Air conditioning", "Wi-Fi", "Kitchen", and "Breakfast". Being thorough here ensures your property appears in filtered searches.

Make sure to check "Pet Friendly" or "Workspace or desk" if applicable, as these are highly sought-after filters for many travelers.

Configuring Location, Pricing, and Policies

The final stage of the listing process moves to location data, financial settings, and the rules guests must follow during their stay.

1

Set the Property Address Click Next to move to the second page of the form. In the Address section, start typing the Street address. FavHost will provide suggestions via an autocomplete menu. Selecting a suggestion will automatically populate the City, Zip, Country, and State fields. A map preview will appear to confirm the location.

2

Establish Pricing and Fees Under the map, locate the Pricing section. Enter the Price/night in USD. You can also specify additional costs such as Taxes and a Cleaning fee. If you require security, enter a Refundable deposit amount.

3

Define Check-In/Out Times and Instructions In the Check in / Check Out section, select the standard Check-in Time and Check-out Time from the dropdown menus. To help guests prepare, you can upload specific instruction documents. Click the upload icons under Upload Check-in Instruction and Upload Check-out Instruction to attach PDFs or image guides.

4

Finalize House Rules and Policies At the bottom of the form, fill in the House Rules and Policies section. Use the text boxes to clearly outline House Rules, Cancellation Policies, and Rental Contract Terms and Conditions. Once all information is accurate, click the Create button at the top-right of the page to publish your listing.

Conclusion

Congratulations! You have successfully created a detailed property listing on FavHost. By providing thorough descriptions, high-quality media, clear amenities, and transparent policies, you've set the stage for a positive guest experience. Your next step should be to review your listing on the Listings page to ensure all information appears correctly to potential guests.

FAQ

  • Can I edit my listing after it has been created?

    Yes, you can navigate to the Listings tab at any time to update descriptions, pricing, photos, or amenities.

  • What file formats are supported for check-in instructions?

    FavHost supports common document formats such as PDF and image files (JPG, PNG) up to 5MB in size.

  • How do I change the currency for my pricing?

    The pricing currency is typically set at the account level. Check your profile or account settings to adjust the default currency for your listings.

  • Why is my listing not showing up in searches?

    Ensure that your 'Listing Status' is set to 'Active'. If it is set to 'Draft' or 'Inactive', it will not be visible to potential guests.

  • Is there a limit to how many photos I can upload?

    While there isn't a strict numerical limit shown, each image should be under 5MB to ensure optimal performance and fast page loading for guests.

  • Can I set different prices for weekends?

    Advanced pricing rules, including weekend rates and seasonal adjustments, can usually be managed through the Calendar or specific pricing settings after the initial listing is created.
