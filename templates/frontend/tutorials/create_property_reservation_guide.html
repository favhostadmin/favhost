Introduction

Use the Listing tab in Favhost to browse properties, filter by date, and finalize bookings through the Create Reservation portal.

Managing property rentals requires a streamlined process for matching guests with available dates and securing their information. Favhost provides a centralized dashboard to handle these tasks efficiently. By navigating through the property listings and utilizing the integrated reservation form, hosts can convert inquiries into confirmed bookings with detailed payment schedules and guest credentials in just a few clicks.

This guide is intended for property managers and administrative staff who need to handle manual booking entries. To follow along, you should have administrative access to your Favhost account and the guest's contact and travel details ready for input.

Browsing and Filtering Listings

The first step in securing a booking is identifying the right property for your guest's requested timeframe. Favhost's listing interface allows you to view all managed properties at a glance and filter them based on availability.

1

Access the Listing Module
From the main Favhost dashboard, locate the top navigation bar. Click on the Listing tab to open the property gallery. This view displays all your active properties with key details like the nightly rate and property title.

2

Apply Date Filters
To ensure you are viewing properties that are actually available, use the date picker at the top of the gallery. Click on the Check-in - Check-out field to open the calendar. Select the start and end dates for the reservation. The system will refresh the list to show only those properties that have no conflicting bookings for that specific range.

Filtering by date early in the process prevents double-bookings and ensures the rates displayed in the summary are accurate for the chosen season.

Creating a New Reservation

Once you have identified an available property, you can proceed to create a formal reservation. Favhost allows you to initiate this from the listing card itself, which pre-populates the property selection in the reservation form.

3

Review Property Details
Hover over the chosen property card and click on the three-dot menu icon. From the dropdown options, select View Detail. This opens a modal window that provides a comprehensive overview of the property, including its location, room credentials (like Wi-Fi passwords), and a breakdown of the payment summary for the selected dates.

4

Initiate the Reservation
At the bottom of the property details modal, or directly from the listing card's menu, click Create Reservation. This action transitions you to the full reservation form where you will enter guest-specific information.

Finalizing Guest and Booking Information

The Create Reservation portal is where you finalize the administrative side of the booking. You must ensure all guest details are accurate to facilitate a smooth check-in process.

5

Enter Guest Details
In the Guest Details section on the left, fill in the guest's first and last name, email address, and phone number. Favhost also provides fields for street address, city, and country if you need to maintain a complete profile for tax or local compliance reasons.

6

Confirm Reservation Dates and Notes
On the right side of the form, verify the Reservation dates. You can also add specific notes in the Notes field—such as "early check-in requested"—and specify the Booking source, such as "Personal Website" or a specific OTA.

You can upload guest photos or identification documents directly into the form by clicking the Guest Photo or Guest IDs/Documents upload zones.

7

Finalize and Create
Scroll to the bottom of the page to review the Payment Summary one last time. This ensures all fees, such as cleaning and application fees, are correctly calculated. Once satisfied, click the orange Create button at the top right of the screen.

Reviewing the Confirmed Reservation

After clicking create, the system processes the request and displays a confirmation message. It is good practice to review the generated reservation record to ensure all automated totals and guest details were saved correctly.

8

Verify Reservation Details
Upon successful creation, you will see a "Reservation created successfully" notification. The screen will refresh to show the Reservation details page. Here, you can see the unique Reservation ID (e.g., #7491), the guest's profile, and a detailed Payment Schedule that outlines when specific payments like cleaning fees or the refundable deposit are due.

Conclusion

You have successfully navigated from browsing listings to finalizing a guest reservation in Favhost. By following these steps, you ensure that property availability is accurately managed and that all necessary guest documentation and payment schedules are centralized. From here, your next step would be to monitor the Calendar tab to see your new booking in context with other reservations or to use the Inquiry module to communicate directly with your guest.

FAQ

  • How do I change the dates after a reservation is created?

    You can edit the reservation by clicking the Action button on the Reservation details page and selecting 'Edit'. Note that changing dates may affect the calculated total if nightly rates vary.

  • Can I add additional fees that are not in the standard summary?

    Yes, the 'Other fees' field in the reservation form allows for manual adjustments or one-time charges specific to a guest's request.

  • What happens if a property is already booked for my chosen dates?

    If a property has a conflict, it will not appear in the filtered results under the Listing tab. If you try to force a booking through another menu, the system will provide a validation error.

  • Where do I find the guest's Wi-Fi password for their stay?

    These details are found in the Address and Room credentials section of the Listing Detail modal or the specific property settings.

  • Is the guest notified automatically when I click Create?

    This depends on your specific Favhost configuration. Typically, an automated confirmation email is triggered to the guest email address provided in the Guest Details section.

  • How do I cancel a reservation I just made?

    Navigate to the Action menu on the Reservation details page and select 'Cancel'. You will likely need to provide a reason for the cancellation for internal tracking.
