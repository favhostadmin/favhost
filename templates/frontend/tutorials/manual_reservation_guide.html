Introduction

Use Favhost to efficiently browse your property listings, verify availability for specific dates, and manually create new guest reservations with full payment and guest detail tracking.

Manually managing bookings is a core part of any short-term rental operation. Whether you're taking a direct booking over the phone or processing a returning guest, Favhost provides a streamlined interface to move from listing discovery to a confirmed reservation in minutes. This guide covers how to filter your properties, inspect listing details, and complete the reservation form to ensure your calendar and financial records stay up to date.

This article is designed for property managers and owners using Favhost. Before starting, ensure you have your listings populated and current guest information ready for entry.

Browsing and Filtering Listings

To begin a new reservation, you first need to identify the correct property and verify its availability for your guest's requested dates. The Listing tab provides a visual grid of all your managed properties with quick-access filters.

1

Filter by Date and Guests

Navigate to the Listing menu at the top of the dashboard. Use the date range picker to select the Check-in and Check-out dates. You can also specify the number of Guests using the dropdown menu. Clicking Filter will refresh the grid to show only those properties that are unbooked and meet your guest capacity requirements for that specific window.

2

Review Property Availability

The property cards display immediate status indicators, such as Active or Available, along with the nightly rate. Scroll through the results to find the specific unit your guest is interested in.

Inspecting Listing Details

Once you have identified a potential property, it is important to review the full listing details to confirm amenities, address information, and the total payment summary before proceeding to create the booking.

1

Open the Property Menu

Hover over the desired property card and click the three-dot menu icon. From the dropdown list, select View Listing. This action opens a comprehensive detail view without leaving the main page.

2

Verify Listing Specifics

Review the Listing Details modal. Here you can find the property's Location, Address, and crucial Room credentials like Wi-Fi passwords. The right-hand sidebar displays the Payment Summary, providing a breakdown of the daily rate, cleaning fees, and refundable deposits for the selected dates.

Always double-check the Payment Summary before clicking Create Reservation. This ensures the guest is quoted the correct total including all mandatory fees and taxes.

Creating a Manual Reservation

After confirming the listing details, clicking Create Reservation opens the booking form. This section is where you input all guest-specific data and finalize the financial terms for the stay.

1

Input Guest Details

In the Guest Details section on the left, enter the primary guest's First name, Last name, Email, and Phone number. Providing a valid email is essential for automated confirmations and guest communication.

2

Configure Reservation and Booking Source

On the right side of the form, verify the Check-in and Check-out dates and times. Use the Booking source dropdown to indicate where this reservation originated (e.g., Personal Website, Phone, or Walk-in). You can also add specific Notes or mention the Purpose of stay (like a birthday or business trip) to help tailor the guest's experience.

3

Review the Final Payment Schedule

Scroll to the bottom of the form to view the Payment Summary and Payment Schedule. This area confirms the total amount due and outlines when specific payments (like cleaning fees or the nightly rate) are scheduled to be collected.

4

Finalize and Save

Once all fields are complete, click the Create button in the top-right corner. A confirmation message ("Reservation created successfully") will appear at the top of the screen, and you will be redirected to the Reservation details page.

Double-check that the Number of guests does not exceed the property's maximum capacity to avoid overbooking or safety violations.

Conclusion

You have successfully navigated the process of identifying an available listing and manually creating a guest reservation in Favhost. By utilizing the date and guest filters, you ensured that the selected property was ready for booking, and by carefully filling out the reservation form, you established a clear record of guest details and payment expectations. You can now view this reservation at any time in your Reservations dashboard to manage check-ins or update payment statuses.

FAQ

  • How do I filter for properties that allow more than two guests?

    Use the Guests dropdown in the top filter bar of the Listing page to set your required capacity before clicking Filter.

  • Can I change the check-in time after the reservation is created?

    Yes, you can navigate to the Reservation details page for that booking and use the Action menu to edit the check-in and check-out parameters.

  • What happens if a property doesn't show up in the search results?

    If a property is missing, ensure it is set to Active status and that there are no conflicting reservations on the calendar for your selected dates.

  • Is it mandatory to provide a guest email?

    While the system may allow you to proceed, providing an email is highly recommended so Favhost can send automated booking confirmations and check-in instructions.

  • Where can I see the breakdown of the cleaning fees?

    The cleaning fee is displayed in the Payment Summary sidebar of the Listing Details modal and again at the bottom of the Create Reservation form.

  • Can I track where my bookings are coming from?

    Yes, use the Booking source dropdown in the reservation form to categorize bookings as coming from your personal website, social media, or phone calls.

  • How do I confirm the reservation is saved?

    Look for the green success banner at the top of the screen immediately after clicking Create; this indicates the data has been persisted to your dashboard.
