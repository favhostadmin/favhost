Frequently asked questions

How long does it take?

Usually around a minute per photo. When you submit several, they’re enhanced in parallel and you’ll see the preview as soon as the first one is ready.

Do I need an account?

No. Upload your photos or paste your listing link and you’ll see your enhanced preview in about a minute.

How many photos can I add?

One, or up to {{ max_per_submit }} at a time — JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB each. There’s a daily limit of {{ daily_limit }} images per visitor.

Will it change what my rooms look like?

No. It corrects perspective, light and colour. It isn’t meant to add, remove or alter furniture or fixtures. As with any AI tool, please check each result before publishing it.

What happens to my photos?

Your photos are sent to an AI provider to be enhanced and stored privately — only your own browsing session can open them. See our Privacy Policy for details.

My link didn’t work — what now?

Some sites block automated access. Choose “Attach images manually” and add your photos directly.
