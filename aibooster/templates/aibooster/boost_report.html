Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No sign-up needed. Paste your listing link and your report is ready in about a minute. You can run up to {{ report_daily_limit }} reports a day.

Which sites does it work with?

Airbnb, Booking.com, other travel sites and FavHost listings. If a site blocks automated access, just paste your listing details instead.

Will it change my listing?

No. The report only gives you suggestions — you decide what to copy into your own listing.

What if you can’t read part of my listing?

We tell you which parts were missing and leave them out of the analysis. We never guess or make up details about your property.

How long does it take?

Usually under a minute. You can leave the page open and your report appears as soon as it’s ready.

Where does my listing text go?

It’s sent to an AI provider to write your report. See our Privacy Policy for details.
